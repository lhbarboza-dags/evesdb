Data Sources

Three commonly used data collection approaches were used in compiling ecosystem service valuation studies for the Philippines and comparable international sites. These approaches include: 1) literature search, 2) mining existing databases, and 3) expert referral. Literature search in electronic journals for existing research on ecosystem services in the Philippines and in comparable international sites for the past 10 years was conducted. Similarly, existing economic valuation databases such as EVRI, the TEEB Valuation Database and now the Ecosystem Services Valuation Database (ESVD), ValuAsia (or EEPSEA), and ENValue, among others were mined. Referrals for relevant case studies, reports, and other publications were also obtained from experts in economic valuation of ecosystem services.
