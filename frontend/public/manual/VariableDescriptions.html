Variable Descriptions

Data Sub-Categories/ Variable Name

Description

Value ID

A unique ID number generated automatically for each ecosystem service (ES) value described in each row in the database.

Study ID

A unique ID number generated automatically for each ES valuation study or source of value estimates captured in the database.

Title

Full title of the ES valuation study

Author

Name of the author(s) of the study (inicate last name followed by full initials of the author; for more than one author, indicate the initials first then the last name for the other authors).

Document Type

The original format of the ES valuation study, e.g., published as an article in a journal, government or non-government report, working paper, conference paper, dissertation/thesis, chapter in a book, magazine, or book.

Publication Year

Year the ES valuation study was published

Source

Details on the source of the study by document type, as follows: 1) Journal or article from a magazine - Name of the journal/magazine, journal/magazine number and volume, page numbers of the article; 2) Government or non-government report - Name of the organization producing the report; 3) Working paper - Government/Faculty series number, Name of the government branch/university department, Name of the government agency/university; 4) Conference paper - Paper presented at the name and location of the conference; 5) Dissertation/Thesis - Dissertation, Dissertation Abstracts International (DAI) number (if available), Name of University; and 6) Chapter in a book or magazine - Location: Name of publisher.

External link

An external link where the study can be downloaded

Country

Name of country or countries where the valuation study was undertaken

Population Density

Population density is the midyear population divided by land area in square kilometers. Indicates whether 1 = low density if < 150 persons per sq. km. or 2 = high density if >150 persons/sq. km. (based on WB 2007 definition).

WB Income Group

Country classification based on World Bank Income Group at year of study

GDP per Capita

GDP per capita at year of study

Site Name

Name of the study site(s)

Location

Location of the study site(s); indicate barangay, municipality, province, or island or group of islands

Latitude

Latitude of the study site in decimal degrees Convert latitude in degrees, minutes and seconds to latitude in decimal degrees: Latitude Degrees + Latitude Minute/60 + Latitude Seconds/3600

Longitude

Longitude of the study site in decimal degrees; Convert longitude in degrees, minutes and seconds to longitude in decimal degrees: - Longitude Degrees - Longitude Minute/60 - Longitude Seconds/3600

Climate

Type of climate in the study site(s)

Protection Status

Adapted from the existing classifications of protection status in the Philippines (NIPAS Act 1992; E-NIPAS Act 2018) and globally (https://www.iucn.org/theme/protected-areas/about/protected-area-categories)

Protected Area Name

If the status of the study site(s) is "protected", indicate the name of the PA (or choose from the list of PAs)

Attributes

Significant attributes of the study site(s) described in terms of environmental amenities which can include "access to clean air or clean water, or the quality of any other environmental good that may reduce adverse health effects for residents or increase their economic welfare. Environmental goods are typically non-market goods, including clean air, clean water, landscape, green transport infrastructure (footpaths, cycleways, greenways, etc.), public parks, urban parks, rivers, mountains, forests, and beaches." Field, Barry C.; Field, Martha K. (2017). Environmental Economics, An Introduction (7 ed.). McGraw-Hill. p. 145-147. ISBN 978-0-07-802189-3.

Scale

Text description of the scale of the study site over which the ecosystem that produces the service is measured. The scale is categorized into: local (municipal/provincial/state); sub-national (regional); national; or multi-country.

Area

Area of the study site to which ES valuation is applied

Area Unit

Original unit of measure used for the area of the study site to which ecosystem service valuation is applied (e.g., hectare, acre, square kilometer, etc.).

Realm

Classification of the realm that is the subject of the value estimate based on the SEEA-EA Ecosystem Type Reference Classification based on the IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology (Keith et al., 2020, Table 3.2).

Realm Desc

Specific terms used to describe or classify the realm that is the subject of the value estimate.

Biome

Classification of the biome that is the subject of the value estimate based on the SEEA-EA Ecosystem Type Reference Classification based on the IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology (Keith et al., 2020, Table 3.2)

Biome Desc

Specific terms used to describe or classify the biome that is the subject of the value estimate.

Ecosystem Functional Group

Classification of the Ecosystem Functional Group (EFG) that is the subject of the value estimate based on the SEEA-EA Ecosystem Type Reference Classification based on the IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology (Keith et al., 2020, Table 3.2)

EFG Desc

Specific terms used to classify the EFG that is the subject of the value estimate.

Ecosystem Service

Classification of the ecosystem service that is the subject of the value estimate based on the SEEA-EA Reference List of Selected Ecosystem Service (Table 6.3 of the UN-SEEA-EA: Final Draft (v.5 Feb. 2021)

ES Desc

Specific terms used to classify or describe the ecosystem service(s) valued

Ecosystem SubService

Classification of the ecosystem sub-service that is the subject of the value estimate based on the SEEA-EA Reference List of Selected Ecosystem Service (Table 6.3 of the UN-SEEA-EA: Final Draft (v.5 Feb. 2021)

ESS Desc

Specific terms used to classify or describe the ecosystem sub-service valued

Valuation Method

Valuation method(s) used in valuing ecosystem services in the study; refer to SEEA-EA methods for estimating exchange values for ES. Additional methods not preferred by SEEA were included.

Val Method Desc

Specific terms for the valuation method used in the study.

Valued Change

A text description of the change in quality or quantity of the ES that is valued in the study.

Valuation Equation/Function

A simple description of the regression equation and its specification. Example: Dependent variable is a function of explanatory variables (or group of variables). Include information on the model specifications (e.g., logit, probit or quadratic).

Economic Measure

Type of economic value measure estimated in the study (e.g., consumer surplus, cost of injury, price, willingness-to-pay, etc.)

Available Study Info

Types of information available from the study (e.g., code documentation, data sets, maps or pictures, questionnaire or questions used, risk assessment or dose-response relationships, description of scenarios or classification schemes, tables)

Survey/Study Info

Information on data collection method; information on survey administration including survey location, date of survey, sample size, response rate, and other information about pre-testing and survey design; and information on the survey sponsor.

Value Type

Type of value generated by the study, e.g., a point estimate - annual mean, annual median, a range, a net present value, capital/stock value, marginal value, an annualized value, a one time payment or WTP value, or aggregate or total economic value.

ESValue - Point

Estimated monetary value(s) for an ecosystem service (or bundle of services) from a specified ecosystem (or complex of ecosystems) derived using a specified valuation method (or multiple methods) - (Note: adopts ESVD definition).

ESValue - Aggregate

An aggregate or total economic value of the ES (or bundle of services) valued in the study site(s).

ESValue - RangeLow

Lower bound value, if a range of ES values is reported

ESValue - RangeHigh

Higher bound value, if a range of ES value is reported

Currency

Currency of the reported monetary value(s)

Unit

Refers to the spatial unit of the reported monetary value(s) ( e.g., per hectare, per km2, per acre, total area, per kilometer, per mile, total length) or the temporal unit of the reported monetary value (e.g., per visit, per day, per month, per year, present value over multiple years) or a combination of spatial and temporal units (e.g., per hectare per year, per household per year)

Value Year

Year of the reported monetary value(s)

PV Years

The time period (in number of years) over which a present value is computed

PV Discount Rate

Discount rate(s) used in converting a stream of future benefits to a present value equivalent

Beneficiary Type

Text description of the type of beneficiaries of the ES (e.g., visitors, households, non-users, tourists, etc.)

Beneficiary Number

Number of beneficiaries that benefit from the ES

Beneficiary Unit

The unit used for beneficiaries for which the ES value estimate is reported (e.g., number of visitors/tourists, number of persons, number of households, etc.

Summary

Provides a concise overview of the information available in the database for each valuation study, addressing key questions such as: What ecosystem service/sub-service is being valued? Where was the study conducted? When was the study conducted? What methods were utilized for data collection and valuation? What is the estimated economic value of the ecosystem service based on the chosen economic measure?
